Our story

From Thomson Rubbers to TJP Mats

Founded in 1988 as Thomson Rubbers by PK Thomas, TJP Mats Pvt Ltd has grown into a globally recognised manufacturer of industrial rubber mats. ISO 9001:2015 certified and a Sedex member, the company exports to Europe, the US, the Middle East and Africa.

Rich Nutrition Sàrl, based in Geneva, is the exclusive Swiss and European distribution partner.

Our dream is to bring the best quality products directly from where they originate to our clients, at the most affordable price.
